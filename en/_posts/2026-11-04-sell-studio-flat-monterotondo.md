> **In short:** To sell a studio flat in Monterotondo you need a price based on similar sales nearby, paperwork in order (title deed, land registry search and floor plan, energy certificate, building compliance) and certainty that the property is genuinely habitable. Studio flats sell quickly but buyers are careful: they look at floor area, running costs and rental yield.

## The studio flat market in Monterotondo

Monterotondo is one of the largest towns north-east of Rome, with Monterotondo Scalo station on the FL1 line. A studio flat close to the transport links suits people who commute to Rome every day and those looking for a small home to let.

## Who buys a studio flat

Buyers come in two kinds: those who want to live in it (singles, young couples, people working away from home) and those who buy to let. For the second group, possible rent, building service charges and ease of letting count; for the first, light, quiet, parking and transport links.

## Is it really a studio flat? Habitability and floor area

A studio flat can be sold as a home only if it meets the requirements for being one. The ministerial decree of 5 July 1975 sets minimum areas for dwellings: for a single room, about 28 m² for one person and 38 m² for two, bathroom included. Height, ventilation and lighting count too. If the property began as a store, garage, cellar or shop and was later converted, the floor plan alone is not enough: you need a regular change of use, a residential land registry category and a habitability certificate. Have a surveyor check these before listing.

## Documents to prepare before selling

- Title deed (purchase, inheritance or gift)
- Up-to-date land registry search and floor plan matching the real state of the property
- Energy performance certificate (APE), mandatory and attached to the deed
- Building and planning compliance and habitability certificate
- Service charges, any extraordinary resolutions and the building rules
- Any current lease and details of a mortgage or charge

## How the price of a studio flat is set

The price is based on actual sales of similar studio flats nearby, not on asking prices. Floor, exposure, state of the systems, balcony or parking, service charges and distance from transport links all count. For small sizes the price per square metre is often higher than for large flats, so comparison should be with studio flats, not homes of a different size.

## Taxes and selling costs

If you sell within five years of buying, the capital gain may be taxable (art. 67 of the TUIR); after five years it generally is not, and special rules apply to property received by inheritance or gift. Notary fees and any agency commission are added at sale. For your case, ask a notary or an accountant.

## What buyers look at in Monterotondo

Buyers of a studio flat in Monterotondo compare several listings at once: the listing must say straight away the floor area, floor, service charges, condition and documents available. A studio flat with everything in order sells faster than one that needs sorting out.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I sell a unit registered as a store or garage as a studio flat?**
No, not until it has residential use and a habitability certificate: the change of use must be regularised with a surveyor, and it is not always possible. Otherwise it is sold for what it is, a store or garage.

**Do I need an energy certificate to sell a studio flat?**
Yes: the energy performance certificate must be drawn up by a qualified technician and attached to the deed of sale.

**Can I sell a studio flat that is let?**
Yes: the lease continues with the new owner. It can be an advantage for a buyer looking for income.

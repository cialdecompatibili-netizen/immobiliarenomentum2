People looking at a home decide in a few minutes whether they like it. First impressions come from the listing photos and from the first moments of the viewing, so preparing the property is the cheapest investment you can make before selling.

## 1. Declutter and tidy up

Fewer objects, more perceived space. Clear surfaces, wardrobes and balconies: visitors must be able to imagine their own life there, not yours.

## 2. Depersonalise

Family photos, collections and very personal objects are distracting. Leave neutral, welcoming rooms.

## 3. More light

Open curtains and shutters, clean the windows, replace dim bulbs. A bright home seems bigger and better cared for.

## 4. Fix the small things

Dripping taps, broken handles, marked walls, blackened grout: these are details buyers notice and that make them think of hidden costs. Fixing them costs little.

## 5. Cleaning and smells

A thorough clean before each viewing and a space without strong smells make a real difference. It is better to air the rooms than to cover smells with intense fragrances.

## 6. Take care of the garden, terrace and entrance

The outside is the first contact. A well-kept garden or a tidy terrace with a few pieces of furniture improves the impression even before going in.

## 7. Professional photos for the listing

Listings are scrolled in a few seconds: bright, landscape-format photos taken in tidy rooms make the difference between a viewing requested and one ignored. For high-end homes, video and virtual tours also work well.

## When home staging is needed

If the home is empty or very dated, a light staging with a few pieces of furniture and neutral textiles helps people understand what each room is for. It is not about redoing the home, but about showing it off for what it is.

Anyone who decides to sell almost always asks the same question: how much can I ask? The answer is neither the price you paid, nor what you would like to get, but what the market is willing to pay today for a home like yours. These are the six factors that weigh the most.

## 1. The area, even down to the street

Two similar homes a few blocks apart can be worth different amounts. What counts is the services within walking distance, the connections with Rome, the noise, the greenery and the reputation of the neighbourhood. For a first reference you can consult the quotations of the Real Estate Market Observatory of the Revenue Agency, bearing in mind that they are average values and not the price of your home.

## 2. The commercial square metres

The price is calculated on the commercial surface, which does not coincide with the usable floor area. Balconies, terraces, garden, cellar and garage are counted with reduced coefficients compared to the main surface. Understanding how your outdoor spaces are weighted avoids surprises in the negotiation.

## 3. Floor, exposure and light

A lift, a high floor, the exposure and the brightness change the perceived value. A bright, quiet home sells better than a dark one of the same size.

## 4. Condition and systems

Systems up to standard, windows in good condition, tidy bathrooms and kitchen: a buyer notices them immediately and, when something is missing, tends to discount more than the real costs. Small repairs before the viewings often pay back more than they cost.

## 5. Energy class

The energy performance certificate is mandatory to sell and the class must be stated in the listings. A high class is a selling point, a low one leads the buyer to ask for a discount for the works.

## 6. The moment and the competition

What counts is how many similar homes are for sale in the area and at what price. A property that is out of the market stays online for months, loses interest and in the end sells anyway, but for less. Setting the right price from the start is almost always the fastest and most profitable way.

## How to get a real estimate

An online listing only gives you an idea. A serious valuation starts from the visit, from the comparison with completed sales in the area and from the check of the property documents.
